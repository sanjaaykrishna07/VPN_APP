Project
Overview
A snapshot of the what, why, and how.

QuantumSafe VPN is an end-to-end post-quantum cryptographic VPN engineered to protect mobile IP communications against Harvest Now, Decrypt Later (HNDL) attacks and future quantum computers running Shor's algorithm. By replacing classical RSA and ECC with finalized NIST standards (FIPS 203 ML-KEM-1024 and FIPS 204 ML-DSA-65), it delivers authenticated, hardware-accelerated AES-256-GCM encrypted tunneling between an unprivileged Android client and a hardened Linux security gateway with automated dynamic IP pooling.

Impact &
Conclusion
Highlights the value and outcome of the project.

QuantumSafe VPN has a strong impact by making quantum-safe mobile networking robust, practical, and highly accessible. Its integration of lattice-based post-quantum cryptography (NIST FIPS 203 & 204) with native Android VpnService proves that smartphones can defend against Harvest Now, Decrypt Later (HNDL) attacks without sacrificing battery life, packet throughput, or user experience. The project sets a solid foundation for future advancements in post-quantum mobile cybersecurity.

Insights &
Results
what the platform detects and delivers.

This project provides deep empirical insights into post-quantum mobile tunneling. Rigorous verification across cryptographic units, wire protocols, and physical testbeds confirmed 100% pass rates across all NIST KAT vectors. Active adversary injection verified zero packet leakage, 100% rejection of bit-flipping attacks via Galois MAC tags, and sub-40ms PQC handshakes with sustained 50+ Mbps throughput on Google Pixel (Android 14).
